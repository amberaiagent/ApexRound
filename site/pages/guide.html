02
QUALIFY

Your access.
5 million $ARENA.

Use Robinhood Chain and hold at least 5,000,000 tokens from the official access contract. Select Check balance to verify your eligibility.

ENTRY THRESHOLD5,000,000 $ARENA

Your access tokens stay in your wallet. A balance check is not a token approval or a transfer.

03
REGISTER

One window.
One explicit entry.

Registration for each later round opens during the current round’s final hour. Connect, verify your balance and choose the registration action before the next round starts.

REGISTRATION1 hour
NEXT ROUND24 hours

Your wallet asks you to sign a message identifying the round and its timing. Check those details and confirm. Wait for the confirmed entry in My arena.

The first launch round used a 30-minute registration window. Later rounds use the one-hour window above.

Check the next entry window
04
COMPETE

Performance
makes the difference.

The competition format is a 24-hour trading round, with return measured in ETH. Qualifying means finishing strictly above +50% and reaching the top 10.

With ten qualifying winners, each receives an equal 10% share of the round pool. Portfolio valuation, eligible trading activity and payment details still require final confirmation.
