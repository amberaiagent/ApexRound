01 / ACCESS & ENTRY

Your wallet is
your starting point.

Entry requires at least 5,000,000 tokens from the official access contract on Robinhood Chain. Connecting your wallet, checking your balance and registering are separate actions.

  • Register during the entry window for the specific round.
  • Sign the round-specific message in your wallet.
  • Your balance is checked before registration is confirmed.
  • Your access tokens stay in your wallet.

02 / THE ROUND CLOCK

24 hours.
A shared starting line.

2 hoursFirst launch registration
1 hourLater registration windows
24 hoursDuration of each round

First registration opens at official token activation and lasts two hours. The first round starts when this window closes. Later rounds follow consecutively. Registration for the next round opens in the current round’s final hour and closes when the next round starts.

Round listings use Eastern Time. The countdown follows the official arena clock.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Earn your place.

The announced qualification target is a trading return strictly above +50%, measured in ETH, and a place among the top 10 qualifying traders.

>50%Exactly +50% does not pass the threshold.

The calculation of portfolio value, eligible assets and the treatment of transfers still need final confirmation. Verified trading returns and rankings are not available yet.

04 / RESULTS & POOL

A shared reward.
Verified first.

With ten qualifying winners, each receives an equal 10% share of the round pool. The stated intention is to allocate 100% of $ARENA fees designated for this program to its round pool.

The fee source, pool amount, payout currency and payment timing have not been finalized. A round reaching its end time does not mean that rankings or payments are final.

Current status

Registration is available during scheduled entry windows. Verified portfolio performance, final rankings and payout records have not been published.

05 / STILL TO BE CONFIRMED

The details matter.

These terms remain open. They should be finalized before relying on a particular activity or reward outcome.

  • Fee source and payout currency
  • Minimum portfolio, eligible assets and trading venues
  • Portfolio valuation and treatment of external transfers
  • Tie-breaking
  • Result review and payment timing
  • Whether 5M $ARENA must be held throughout the round
  • Distribution with fewer than ten or no qualifiers
  • Whether $ARENA is included in the trading portfolio
